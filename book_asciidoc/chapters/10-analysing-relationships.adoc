= Chapter 10: Analysing Relationships

[role="tts-voice-echo"]
Chapter Ten: Analysing Relationships

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
Putting together concepts is basic to thinking (Lessons 1-9). Pulling them apart (**analysis**) is just as vital. When faced with raw data or information, we have to break it down (**analyse**) it.

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
The questions in this exercise are often found in intelligence tests.

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
Good thinkers are quick to analyse the relationship between smaller things and the larger thing that they are part of.

== Example 1

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
Consider this relationship: Feathers are to a bird as scales are to what?

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
In order to find the answer, we have to first find out, or analyse, how feathers and bird are related. How are feathers related to a bird? Answer: feathers cover the body of a bird. So to complete the relationship, we need to find what covers the body of a fish. The answer is scales.

image::image-16.png[Feathers to bird relationship diagram]

== Example 2

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
Blue is to colour as eagle is to what?

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
Blue is an **example** of a colour. Eagle is an **example** of a bird.

=== Useful questions to ask when ANALYSING RELATIONSHIPS

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
Here are helpful questions for understanding relationships:

* How is the second thing related to the first thing, e.g. size, colour, use, material, part, shape, example of..?

* What is related to C, or the third thing, in this same way?
