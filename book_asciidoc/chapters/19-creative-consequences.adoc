Chapter Nineteen: Creative Consequences

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
Creative thinkers often ask the question "What would happen if ...?" when they are trying to come up with creative ideas or solutions.

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
To answer this question they have to imagine the consequences of some change. This way of thinking is used by inventors, writers, scientists, people in business, and ordinary people who want to think more creatively about problems they face.

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
Here are some examples of the kind of thinking that you are about to practice:

== Examples

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
If there were no more TREES in the world then... there would be less oxygen in the air ... and this would mean many people would die in the big cities.

image::image-42.png[Trees and oxygen diagram]

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
If we ran out of ELECTRICITY... then it would mean the end of computers, television and all but face to face communication.

image::image-43.png[Electricity consequences diagram]

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
If I decided not to get ANGRY again, no matter what... then I might make more friends, and I might be happier.

== Possible Answers

[role="tts-voice-alloy"]
Here are some example answers to guide your thinking:

1. If there were no more birds in the world then there would be more insects and this would mean more leaves of plants eaten.

2. If the Earth no longer had a moon then no more tides and this would mean the bottom of ocean not stirred for fish to feed.

3. If there was no longer any oil deposits on Earth then no petrol could be made and this would mean that cars would become useless.

4. If there was no longer any whales on Earth then no longer whale hunters and this would mean that some countries would have to find other meat to eat and other chemicals that come from whale oil.
