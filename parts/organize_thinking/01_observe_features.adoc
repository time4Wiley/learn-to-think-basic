= Observing Properties

[TTS]
....
Welcome to the first chapter on observing properties. In this lesson, we'll learn how to look at things more carefully and understand why they are designed the way they are.
....

[.lead]
Everything around us, whether made by humans or nature, has a specific design for a reason.

== Understanding Design Purpose

[TTS]
....
Let's explore why things are designed the way they are. Every object's properties serve a specific purpose.
....

[quote]
____
The design or composition of any given thing is special and is that way for a particular reason. Bottles don't just happen to be made of glass rather than other materials. Stop lights don't just happen to be red rather than other colours. And trees don't just happen to have thousands of leaves rather than 10 or 20.
____

== The SCUMPS Method

[TTS]
....
To help you observe properties effectively, we use the SCUMPS method. Each letter stands for a different property to look for.
....

[NOTE]
====
*SCUMPS* helps you focus on key properties:

* *S*ize
* *C*olor
* *U*se
* *M*aterial
* *P*arts
* *S*hape
====

== Example Analysis

[TTS]
....
Let's look at an example of how to analyze an object using the SCUMPS method. We'll examine a brick and its properties.
....

.Properties of a Brick
[cols="1,2"]
|===
|Property |Reason for the Property

|Rough surface
|Cement sticks to it easily

|Heavy weight
|Wind won't blow it away

|Geometric shape
|Easy to stack in rows
|===

== Self-Reflection Questions

[TTS]
....
As you observe objects, ask yourself these important questions to deepen your understanding.
....

[qanda]
What size does this object have?:: Why is this size appropriate for its purpose?
What color is it?:: How does the color help it function?
What is it used for?:: Could it be used differently with different properties?
What is it made of?:: Why was this material chosen?
What parts does it have?:: How do the parts work together?
What shape is it?:: How does the shape help it function?

[IMPORTANT]
====
Always ask yourself: "Why does this thing have these specific properties rather than other possibilities?"
====
