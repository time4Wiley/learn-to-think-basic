== Understanding Generalization

[TTS]
....
Think about all the birds you've seen in books or real life. Despite their differences, you probably have a general picture in your mind of what makes something a bird - things like feathers, beaks, eggs, and nests. This mental picture is your generalization about birds.
....

=== Why We Generalize

[TTS]
....
Generalizations are like templates or models that guide our thinking. They help us:
* Recognize new examples of things we know
* Predict what new things might be able to do
* Organize our knowledge efficiently
* Make connections between different things
....

== Practice Activities

[TTS]
....
Let's try some activities to help you practice making good generalizations.
....

1. Animal Categories:
* List 5 different animals
* Write their properties
* Find common features
* Make a generalization

2. Classroom Objects:
* Choose a category (like "writing tools")
* List examples
* Find shared properties
* Test your generalization

== Self-Reflection Questions

[TTS]
....
As you practice making generalizations, ask yourself these important questions.
....

[qanda]
Can I picture five or more examples?:: Make sure you have enough examples
What features do they share?:: Look for common properties
Are there any exceptions?:: Test your generalization
How can this help me?:: Think about practical applications

[IMPORTANT]
====
Good generalizations help us:
* Learn new things faster
* Make predictions
* Organize knowledge
* Understand patterns
* Solve problems
====
