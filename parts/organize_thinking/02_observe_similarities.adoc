== Using SCUMPS for Similarity Analysis

[TTS]
....
Remember SCUMPS from Chapter 1? We can use this same tool to find similarities between objects. Let's look at each aspect:
Size - Are they similar in size?
Color - Do they share any colors?
Use - Are they used in similar ways?
Material - Are they made of similar materials?
Parts - Do they have similar parts?
Shape - Do they share any shape characteristics?
....

.Example: Comparing a Banana and a Lemon
[cols="1,2"]
|===
|Common Properties |Explanation

|Yellow color
|Both fruits have yellow skin when ripe

|Food
|Both are edible fruits used for nutrition

|Thick skin
|Both have protective outer layers

|Grow on trees
|Both are tree fruits
|===

== Suggested Answers

[TTS]
....
Let's look at some possible similarities for each pair. Remember, you might find other valid similarities too!
....

.Flowers and Birds
* Both are living things
* Both need sunlight, air, and water
* Both come in different types and colors

.Roads and Rivers
* Both have names
* Both transport things from one place to another
* Both have starting points and destinations

.Chairs and Horses
* Both can be sat upon
* Both have four legs
* Both come in different heights and colors

.Doors and Books
* Both are made from trees (wood/paper)
* Both are rectangular
* Both can be opened and closed

.Numbers 4 and 9
* Both are single digits
* Both have exact square roots
* Both divide evenly into 36

.Words "fell" and "ran"
* Both are verbs
* Both have one vowel
* Both are past tense forms

.Squares and Circles
* Both are closed shapes
* Both are two-dimensional
* Both are geometric figures
