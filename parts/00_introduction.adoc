Teaching children how to think is as important as teaching them what to think about.

== The Importance of Teaching Thinking Skills

[TTS]
....
Students need both content to think about and the thinking processes to analyze that content. Let's understand why these skills are crucial for young learners.
....

Students need two essential components in their education:

1. Content to think about
2. Thinking processes to analyze that content

Most importantly, they need to learn good questions to ask themselves when thinking about content in different ways. This concept of 'metacognition' (thinking about thinking) empowers students by giving them a wide repertoire of useable cognitive tools.

[NOTE]
====
While curriculum planners often assume that students naturally learn these processes, research shows this is not the case.
====

== Core Thinking Processes

[TTS]
....
We regularly use about twenty basic thinking processes to connect and make sense of information. These core processes form the foundation of effective learning and problem-solving.
....

The book focuses on four main types of thinking:

* Organizational Thinking
* Analytical Thinking
* Evaluative Thinking
* Creative Thinking

These processes cover various subjects including:

* Mathematics
* Language
* Social Studies
* Science

== How to Use This Book

[TTS]
....
Each lesson in this book follows a structured format designed to maximize learning. Let's understand how to use these lessons effectively.
....

Each lesson contains:

1. *Introductory Notes* +
The first page of each lesson contains notes for teachers to discuss with pupils.

2. *Working Examples* +
Teachers can work through these examples to demonstrate what is expected in the exercises.

3. *Student Worksheets* +
Once students understand the thinking process, they can work through the items on their worksheets.

4. *Suggested Answers* +
Guidelines for evaluating student responses.

5. *Self-Reflection Questions* +
Questions for pupils to ask themselves when thinking in different ways.

[TIP]
====
The lists of processing questions can be thought of as mental thinking programs for comparing, categorizing, distinguishing facts from opinions, generalizing, and more.
====

== Age Appropriateness

[TTS]
....
This book is specifically designed for children aged six to eleven years old, providing a comprehensive thinking skills program suitable for their developmental stage.
....

The exercises in this book are carefully crafted to:

* Match the cognitive development of children aged 6-11
* Build progressively more complex thinking skills
* Engage students through age-appropriate examples
* Encourage independent thinking and problem-solving

[IMPORTANT]
====
This book provides enough exercises to serve as the foundation of a complete thinking skills program for primary school pupils.
====
